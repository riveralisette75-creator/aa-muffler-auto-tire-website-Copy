About our shop

Experienced automotive care you can trust.

At A&A Muffler Auto & Tire, we're committed to providing dependable automotive service backed by over 14 years of hands-on industry experience from our owner.

As we begin this new chapter, our goal is to bring that knowledge and experience to our community through quality workmanship, honest communication, and a commitment to customer satisfaction.

From exhaust repairs and catalytic converters to brake service, engine diagnostics, tune-ups, oil changes, and tires, we strive to help keep your vehicle running safely and reliably.

Thank you for choosing A&A Muffler Auto & Tire. We look forward to earning your trust and serving you!
